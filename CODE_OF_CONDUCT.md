# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the **Contributor Covenant, version 2.1** as its
code of conduct. The canonical text is available at:

<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>

By participating in this community — issues, pull requests,
discussions, or any other channel — you agree to follow it.

## Expected behavior

- Treat everyone with respect. Disagree with ideas, not people.
- Assume good intent; ask for clarification before reacting.
- Prefer concrete, evidence-backed feedback over personal opinion.
- Make space for people who are new to the project or the technology.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Personal attacks, harassment, or any form of discriminatory conduct.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, reviews, or issues.
- Publishing others' private information without their explicit
  permission.
- Anything that would be inappropriate in a professional engineering
  setting.

## Reporting

If you witness or experience behaviour that violates this policy,
report it privately to **conduct@bouclier.ai**. Reports are read by
the project maintainers only. We will acknowledge receipt within two
business days, investigate confidentially, and take action
proportional to the impact and intent — ranging from a private
warning to a permanent ban from the project.

The maintainers are responsible for enforcement and may remove,
edit, or reject any contribution that does not align with this code
of conduct.

## Scope

This code applies to all project spaces — the repository, issue
tracker, discussions, pull-request reviews, official social
accounts, and any in-person or online event affiliated with the
project.
